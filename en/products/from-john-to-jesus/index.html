What is From John to Jesus?

From John to Jesus is SAGA’s direction for Bible texts and visual storytelling. It offers an entry for readers interested in biblical people, stories and cultural context, keeping the narrative connected to its sources.

People and stories can be a starting point, while versions, translations and traditions require separate consideration. Visual storytelling is not itself historical evidence. This guide does not claim every planned item is complete; consult the independent site for its actual content and sources.

Before you begin

How should I move from a story to its source?

Identify the people and passage, then inspect the version and source notes. Read the original text, historical context and later visual expression as distinct layers.

Does SAGA create an account or purchase for me?

No. The link opens an independent site. Check its current functionality, account, price and privacy information before proceeding.
