What is PLOT?

PLOT is an independent product for self and relationship reflection. Its starting point is a question inside a relationship: separating events, feelings, assumptions and communication needs so you can consider what you can observe and choose.

Useful starting questions include what happened, how you interpreted it, which information is missing and what you would like to express. This introduction offers no psychological diagnosis or guarantee about someone else’s intentions. Check PLOT for available features and account information.

Before you begin

Does PLOT reveal what another person really thinks?

Inference is not fact. Relationship reflection should help organise available information, identify uncertainty and consider your own communication, not claim to read minds.

Does SAGA create an account or purchase for me?

No. The link opens an independent site. Check its current functionality, account, price and privacy information before proceeding.
