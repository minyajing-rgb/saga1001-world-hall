What is Dharma Atlas?

Dharma Atlas is a knowledge-exploration entry for Buddhist texts and culture. It connects texts, people, places and stories, inviting readers to follow sources rather than reduce complex traditions to a single unattributed conclusion.

When reading, check the text version, translator, historical context and interpretive tradition. SAGA introduces the destination; verify citations and page versions within Dharma Atlas. Historical accounts, religious practice and personal interpretation should remain distinguishable.
