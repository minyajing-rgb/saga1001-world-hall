SAGA / COLLABORATION

Start with a real project.

Bring a clear audience, goal, contribution and expected deliverable.

Product and distribution

Independent consumer experiences, interactive products and international distribution. Include the product’s current stage and what each party would deliver.

Original content and IP

Cards, stories, cultural content and cross-media collaboration, with clear ownership and usage rights.

What should an initial proposal include?

Introduce the product or organisation, the audience you want to reach, the specific collaboration and what you can contribute. Include the expected deliverables, schedule, budget boundary and who can make the decision. Public product examples are more useful at this stage than private customer records.

What happens after an enquiry?

The contact link opens your email client; it does not submit a form or commit either party to a project. Scope, responsibilities, rights and commercial terms need separate agreement. A programme-selection record is not a signed partnership and should not be presented as one.

Knowledge and industry exchange

Research questions, source-led content, interviews and industry talks. A discussion or event invitation is not automatically an institutional partnership.
