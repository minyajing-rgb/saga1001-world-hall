小蘑菇忘记了什么

What Did Little Mushroom Forget?

公开前 8 页文字试读 · The first eight story lines
  1. 01

    小蘑菇带着空白小本子，从 W 形的花花星出发了。

    Little Mushroom left W-shaped Blossom Star with an empty notebook.

  2. 02

    他穿过星海，落在地球的一片叶子上。

    He crossed a sea of stars and landed on a leaf on Earth.

  3. 03

    他睡了一觉，醒来却忘了自己的任务。

    He took a nap—and woke up without a clue about his mission.

  4. 04

    “我一定是来干大事的！”他把帽子扶正。

    “I must be here to do something big!” he said, straightening his cap.

  5. 05

    他帮蚂蚁搬糖，替云朵数雨，还给石头排了队。

    He carried sugar for ants, counted raindrops, and lined up pebbles.

  6. 06

    忙了一整天，他的小本子还是一片空白。

    After a whole day of work, his notebook was still empty.

  7. 07

    一只小花精落在他的帽檐上，指了指小本子。

    A flower sprite landed on his cap and pointed to the notebook.

  8. 08

    “你还记得，为什么带它来吗？”

    “Do you remember why you brought it?”

FREE PREVIEW · VOL 02

小蘑菇和海的回信

Little Mushroom and a Letter from the Sea

公开前 8 页文字试读 · The first eight story lines
  1. 01

    小蘑菇把问题写在沙上：“海浪也会做梦吗？”

    Little Mushroom wrote in the sand, “Do waves dream too?”

  2. 02

    他和小花精坐在一旁，等海回信。

    He and Flower Sprite sat nearby, waiting for a reply.

  3. 03

    一阵浪花跑过来，把字全擦掉了。

    A wave rushed over and washed every word away.

  4. 04

    “它的橡皮擦也太大了吧！”小蘑菇嘀咕。

    “That is a very big eraser!” Little Mushroom grumbled.

  5. 05

    小花精翻开本子：“要不要换个办法看看？”

    Flower Sprite opened the notebook: “Shall we try another way?”

  6. 06

    潮水退去，一枚小贝壳留在浅浅的水里。

    The tide slipped back, leaving a small shell in a pool.

  7. 07

    一只小螃蟹从贝壳旁横着走过。

    A little crab sidled past the shell.

  8. 08

    它的脚印像一串歪歪扭扭的小字。

    Its footprints looked like a line of wobbly letters.
