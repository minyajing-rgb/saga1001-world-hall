## Current direction

Use the FIRST simple international visual direction as the basis for future development. Human chibi face, clean short curved eye indication plus simple pupils, white wave-shaped hair, long ears, violet cape, limited bright accents, generous white space. Avoid oversized manga irises, elaborate individual eyelashes, floral tiaras, ornate costume detail and decorative overload. The second more elaborate Chibi series is a retained exploration, not the chosen brand master.
